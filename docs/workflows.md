# TSSE Newsletter Production Workflow

## Current Workflow

### Step 1: New Episode Published

A new episode is published on the TSSE website.

The episode transcript becomes the source of truth for newsletter production.

---

### Step 2: Theme Identification

Review the transcript and identify:

* Primary theme
* Supporting themes
* Founder lessons
* Memorable quotes

The theme becomes the foundation of the newsletter issue.

---

### Step 3: Podcast Entries Section

Create a summary of the new episode.

Objectives:

* Highlight key insights.
* Introduce the guest.
* Emphasize practical lessons for founders and investors.
* Encourage readers to listen to the full episode.

---

### Step 4: Connected Insights Selection

Review approved Rank 4 guests.

Selection Criteria:

* Similar topic or theme.
* Strong complementary perspective.
* Not recently featured in the newsletter.

Objective:

Expand the discussion introduced by the new episode.

---

### Step 5: Startup Corner Selection

Review approved startup watchlist.

Preferred Selection Criteria:

1. Startup has a verified shared investment connection with both Gopi Rangan and the guest.
2. Startup aligns with episode theme.
3. Startup operates in the same industry.
4. Startup has meaningful recent news.

If no strong thematic connection exists:

Select the startup with the strongest recent development.

Avoid startups featured recently.

---

### Step 6: Community Involvement Section

Identify nonprofit, foundation, or community initiative mentioned by the guest.

Research:

* Mission
* Impact
* Recent milestones

Create a concise summary.

---

### Step 7: Email Intro Creation

The intro serves as Gopi Rangan's opening letter.

There is no fixed format.

Possible styles include:

### Style A: Big-Idea Intro

Lead with a broad observation or question.

Example:
"What if the future of leadership looks less like command-and-control and more like empathy and human connection?"

---

### Style B: Founder/Investor Commentary

Lead with an industry trend or misconception.

Example:
"Remember when everyone thought InsurTech would revolutionize insurance overnight?"

---

### Style C: Personal Reflection

Lead with a personal observation or experience.

Example:
"My career journey is rooted in deep tech..."

---

Three alternatives should be generated whenever possible.

---

### Step 8: Add Upcoming Events

Include upcoming events involving Gopi Rangan, Sure Ventures, TSSE, or related activities.

---

### Step 9: Assemble Newsletter Draft

Combine:

* Email Intro
* Podcast Entries
* Connected Insights
* Startup Corner
* Community Involvement

Ensure all sections support a coherent central theme.

---

### Step 10: Editorial Review

Review by:

* Joe Gakere
* Gopi Rangan

Possible edits:

* Tone
* Storytelling
* Featured startup
* Connected Insights selection
* Intro selection

---

### Step 11: Publish

Newsletter is distributed to subscribers and archived for future reference.

---

## Future Workflow

Future versions of TSSE Editorial OS should automate:

* Transcript analysis
* Theme extraction
* Connected Insights recommendations
* Startup Corner recommendations
* Community involvement extraction
* Intro generation

while preserving human editorial oversight.
