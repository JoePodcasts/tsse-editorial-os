Scott Barclay shares insights on when to play an active role as a venture capital investor. He explains with examples how engagement with an entrepreneur starts and eventually disproves or strengthens his conviction in a business idea. 

Highlights: 

  • The right VC:CEO fit is magical.
  • A good investor does not impose his alpha role but is always ready to intervene and lead.
  • Become a student of economics and learn about unrelated markets to sharpen your investment acumen.
  • Entrepreneurial journeys are broad ranging. Founders come in many shapes and forms. Hot referrals and cold-emails are both  good leads.
  • Non-profit: Ecumenical Hunger Program
