Dan Borok, Co-Founder and Managing Partner of NVP Capital, discusses opportunities for founders building innovative solutions in under-digitized critical industries, including manufacturing, logistics, transportation, energy, and healthcare. Drawing on his experience at Google and in venture capital, Dan explains why NVP backs mission-driven founders tackling complex industry problems. He shares how the firm discovers startups, evaluates investment opportunities, and helps founders navigate enterprise customers through its corporate network.
