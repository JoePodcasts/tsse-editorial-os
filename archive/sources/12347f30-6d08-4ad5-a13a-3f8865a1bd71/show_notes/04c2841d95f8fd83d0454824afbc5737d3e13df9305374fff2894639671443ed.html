Dan Borok, Co-Founder and Managing Partner of NVP Capital, discusses opportunities for founders building innovative solutions in under-digitized critical industries, including manufacturing, logistics, transportation, energy, and healthcare. Drawing on his experience at Google and in venture capital, Dan explains why NVP backs mission-driven founders tackling complex industry problems. He shares how the firm discovers startups, evaluates investment opportunities, and helps founders navigate enterprise customers through its corporate network. Dan also takes listeners inside NVP's investment process, explaining why strong founders sometimes receive a no.

About Dan Borok
Dan Borok is Co-Founder and Managing Partner of NVP Capital, an early-stage venture capital firm investing in mission-critical industries. He began his career at Google before moving into venture investing at Millennium Technology Value Partners. In 2020, he co-founded NVP Capital with Vaughn Crowe to back entrepreneurs transforming industries such as manufacturing, logistics, transportation, energy, and healthcare. Dan is based in New York and serves on the board of Tech.

About NVP Capital
NVP Capital is a New York-based early-stage venture capital firm investing in founders building innovative solutions for under-digitized, mission-critical industries. Founded in 2020 by Dan Borok and Vaughn Crowe, the firm focuses on pre-seed and seed-stage startups transforming manufacturing, logistics, transportation, energy, healthcare, and other essential sectors. Through its NVP Galaxy network of more than 35 corporate partners, the firm helps portfolio companies gain industry insights, navigate go-to-market challenges, and connect with potential enterprise customers.
