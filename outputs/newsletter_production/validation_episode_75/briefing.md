## Founder Lessons

- Show the personal journey and motivation behind the company, not only the product facts.
- In regulated markets, balance risk-taking with respect for operational and compliance complexity.
- If you are an outsider, go deep enough to understand the industry better than insiders who are merely adjacent.
- Seek investors whose focus and ability to help match the company, not just investors willing to wire money.
