## How many bets make a fund?

Portfolio size expresses a view about where exceptional returns come from.

A portfolio can spread the risk of missing a rare winner or give a small number of investments enough weight to change the outcome. The same choice looks different to a direct investor, a fund-of-funds manager and an institution with a small venture allocation.

Which layer is being diversified, and what would count as success at that layer?

## Knowing the founder before the round

Time together can give each side evidence about the partnership it is considering.

Introductions, small pieces of advice and repeated meetings give founders a way to see an investor at work. Investors use the same interval to learn how a team thinks. The evidence ranges from relationships spanning years to cases where prior market knowledge can compress the decision.

What did the founder learn about the investor before accepting a check?
